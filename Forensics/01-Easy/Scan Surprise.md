# Scan Surprise Writeup

> I've gotten bored of handing out flags as text. Wouldn't it be cool if they were an image instead?
> You can download the challenge files here:
> [challenge.zip](https://artifacts.picoctf.net/c_atlas/3/challenge.zip)

## Solution

```shell
wget "https://artifacts.picoctf.net/c_atlas/3/challenge.zip"
```

```shell
unzip challenge.zip
```

Here we Have a `QR code` image, So it's easy scan it with your phone and you got the flag.
And if you don't have a phone you can use `CyberChef` as always.

```text
picoCTF{p33k_@_b00_a81f0a35}
```
